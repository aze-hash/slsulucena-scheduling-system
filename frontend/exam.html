Exam Scheduling Guide

×

Follow these steps to generate and manage examination schedules.

  1. Filter Academic Term & Section Enter the Academic Year (e.g. 2026-2027), choose Semester, Program, Major, Year Level, and Section. Click the + button to add a new section code if needed.
  2. Set Exam Dates & Exam Type Pick one or more examination dates using the date picker and choose the Exam Type (Preliminary, Midterm, or Final).
  3. Load Curriculum Subjects Click Load Subjects to populate subjects from the university curriculum.
  4. Generate Exam Schedule Click Generate Exam Schedule. The system automatically assigns exam dates, time slots, lecture/lab rooms, and faculty proctors while avoiding time overlaps and double-bookings.
  5. Review, Save & Publish Review the timetable in the preview modal, then click Save as Draft or Publish Exam Schedule to make examination schedules available to students and faculty.

Tip: You can reopen this guide anytime by clicking the ⓘ button at the top-right of this page.
