Class Scheduling Guide

×

Follow these steps to generate and manage class schedules.

  1. Set Filters & Add Sections Enter the Academic Year (e.g. 2026-2027), then choose the Semester, Program, Major, Year Level, and Section. Click the + button beside the Section dropdown to add a new section code (e.g. BINDTECH-CPT 1A).
  2. Load Subjects Click Load Subjects to display curriculum subjects (Code, Name, Units, Lecture/Lab hours) for the selected program, major, and year level in the Subjects table.
  3. Generate Schedule Click Generate Schedule. The system automatically assigns days, times, and lecture/laboratory rooms while avoiding section time overlaps and room double-bookings.
  4. Review & Save Inspect the generated schedule in the preview modal, then click Save Schedule -Saved schedules appear in the Saved Schedules list and sync to Firestore.
  5. Edit Schedules with Conflict Validation Click Edit on any saved schedule card to modify subject days, times, or rooms. The system performs automated conflict checks to prevent room double-bookings and section overlaps before saving to Firestore.
  6. Archive or Delete Use Move to Archive to transfer all active saved schedules to the Schedule Archive, or use Delete on individual cards (or Delete All) to remove schedules.

Tip: You can reopen this guide anytime by clicking the ⓘ button at the top-right of this page.
