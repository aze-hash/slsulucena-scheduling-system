About

Discover the Automated Scheduling System of Southern Luzon State University Lucena Campus — designed to simplify class and examination scheduling, reduce conflicts, and support a more efficient academic planning process.

Who We Are

The Automated Scheduling System of Southern Luzon State University Lucena Campus is a web-based platform designed to make academic scheduling faster, smarter, and more organized. It automates the creation of class and examination schedules while considering faculty availability, room assignments, sections, and scheduling conflicts — helping the university save time and improve the overall academic planning process.

🎯

Our Mission

To simplify and improve academic scheduling at Southern Luzon State University – Lucena Campus through a reliable and automated platform that helps chairpersons, department heads, and administrators efficiently manage class and examination schedules.

🔭

Our Vision

To provide a trusted and efficient scheduling system that supports better academic planning, minimizes scheduling conflicts, and promotes effective management of faculty, rooms, sections, and examinations across SLSU Lucena Campus.
