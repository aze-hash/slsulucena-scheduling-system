About the System

Learn about the Automated Scheduling System of Southern Luzon State University — how it began, what drives it, and how it serves our academic community.

Who We Are

The Automated Scheduling System is a university-wide platform developed to address the challenges of manual timetable creation. By automating class and examination scheduling, the system ensures that faculty, rooms, and students are assigned efficiently — reducing conflicts, saving time, and improving academic planning.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide a reliable, efficient, and user-friendly scheduling platform that supports the academic operations of Southern Luzon State University, enabling faculty, chairpersons, and administrators to focus more on education and less on administrative burden.

🔭

Our Vision

To become the standard automated scheduling solution across the university, known for accuracy, innovation, and excellence in academic resource management.
